# School Project Design — Theme Library

## 9. The 15-theme library

Choose one theme based on subject, requested mood, and recent-theme history. Each theme needs a distinctive palette, typography, composition, grid, decoration, and cover treatment. Palette suggestions are starting points, not strict requirements.

### 1. Art Deco — geometric luxury
- **Visual signature:** symmetry, stepped forms, sunbursts, fan shapes, arches, metallic-looking linework.
- **Palette:** black, ivory, gold, emerald, or burgundy.
- **Typography:** elegant high-contrast display serif or geometric display; compact uppercase labels.
- **Layout/grid:** centred hero with symmetrical side panels; framed writing boxes; strong vertical axis.
- **Cover composition:** large title inside a stepped frame or arch, one central object illustration.
- **Decoration:** fine gold-like lines, rays, geometric corner ornaments; keep them outside handwriting areas.
- **Best subjects:** history, literature, business, architecture, chemistry when a polished formal look is desired.
- **Avoid:** excessive simulated glitter, crowded ornament, or fake embossed text that harms readability.

### 2. Bauhaus — functional geometry
- **Visual signature:** circles, triangles, rectangles, primary-colour blocks, disciplined asymmetry.
- **Palette:** red, blue, yellow, black, white/cream.
- **Typography:** geometric sans-serif, strong weights, simple labels.
- **Layout/grid:** modular rectangles, asymmetric balance, clear alignment; use circles as visual anchors.
- **Cover composition:** big title block paired with one geometric construction or object illustration.
- **Decoration:** simple lines, dots, shapes, minimal outlines.
- **Best subjects:** mathematics, physics, technology, engineering, design.
- **Avoid:** ornamental floral framing or random shapes with no relation to the grid.

### 3. Art Nouveau — organic elegance
- **Visual signature:** flowing curves, stems, leaves, blossoms, graceful asymmetry, natural silhouettes.
- **Palette:** sage, olive, dusty pink, cream, muted gold.
- **Typography:** elegant serif/display lettering paired with a simple readable label face.
- **Layout/grid:** organic outer frame around a clear rectangular writing grid.
- **Cover composition:** botanical or nature-related illustration rising beside or behind a protected title area.
- **Decoration:** vines, petals, curved lines, fine botanical line art.
- **Best subjects:** biology, plants, environment, poetry, literature.
- **Avoid:** vines crossing writing boxes or overcomplicated borders that consume space.

### 4. Swiss International Style — rigorous clarity
- **Visual signature:** strong alignment, asymmetry, disciplined spacing, minimal decoration.
- **Palette:** white, black, greys, with one red/cobalt accent.
- **Typography:** neutral sans-serif, clear size hierarchy, restrained bold labels.
- **Layout/grid:** strict grid; broad margins; well-aligned columns and captions.
- **Cover composition:** oversized left-aligned title, small metadata line, one precise image block.
- **Decoration:** rules, numbering, subtle colour bars, tiny markers.
- **Best subjects:** mathematics, computer science, economics, formal reports, any text-heavy subject.
- **Avoid:** unnecessary gradients, shadows, and decorative clutter.

### 5. Japanese Minimalism — calm and intentional
- **Visual signature:** negative space, natural textures, restrained line work, quiet asymmetry.
- **Palette:** warm white, ink black, muted red, bamboo/sage green.
- **Typography:** restrained serif or clean sans-serif; generous spacing.
- **Layout/grid:** simple open layout, few large writing areas, subtle asymmetric balance.
- **Cover composition:** one small, well-composed symbol or illustration offset from the title.
- **Decoration:** brush-inspired linework, soft circles, simple leaves or waves.
- **Best subjects:** geography, environmental studies, literature, reflective topics.
- **Avoid:** filling all empty space. Negative space is an intentional part of this theme.

### 6. Victorian Scientific Journal — archival science
- **Visual signature:** engraved specimens, thin rules, specimen labels, marginal notes, archival paper feel.
- **Palette:** parchment, sepia, dark green, burgundy, charcoal.
- **Typography:** traditional book serif for titles and readable compact labels.
- **Layout/grid:** framed specimen plate beside or above wide writing boxes; small numbered callouts.
- **Cover composition:** one historically plausible scientific specimen or instrument with a clean title plaque.
- **Decoration:** delicate crosshatching, botanical engravings, ruled dividers, small archive-like numbers.
- **Best subjects:** biology, chemistry, medicine, scientific history.
- **Avoid:** overly dark aged paper, fake microtext, scientifically incorrect anatomy or apparatus.

### 7. Space-Age Futurism — retro science future
- **Visual signature:** orbits, star charts, planets, modular panels, smooth rounded forms, technical diagram cues.
- **Palette:** midnight navy, cream/white, silver-grey, orange or cyan accent.
- **Typography:** geometric futuristic display plus plain readable labels.
- **Layout/grid:** modular instrument-panel cards, orbit curves used as subtle connectors.
- **Cover composition:** one central space/science object and a high-contrast title band.
- **Decoration:** stars, fine orbit lines, small labels used sparingly.
- **Best subjects:** astronomy, physics, space, future technology.
- **Avoid:** covering the page with stars, fake control-panel text, or using the theme for unrelated topics without a clear conceptual link.

### 8. Pop Art / Comic Book — loud and playful
- **Visual signature:** thick outlines, halftone dots, comic panels, bursts, speech bubbles.
- **Palette:** yellow, red, cyan/blue, black, white.
- **Typography:** heavy display lettering and straightforward block labels.
- **Layout/grid:** comic-style panels or stacked boxes; vary panel size while keeping the writing areas large.
- **Cover composition:** headline in a dramatic burst or panel plus one clear topical illustration.
- **Decoration:** dots, bursts, simple action lines, speech bubbles kept out of writing space.
- **Best subjects:** road safety, awareness campaigns, health, language, civics.
- **Avoid:** too many competing bursts, unreadable comic fonts, or a page so saturated that handwriting disappears.

### 9. Constructivism — dynamic and historical
- **Visual signature:** diagonal bars, circles, arrows, strong contrast, deliberate motion.
- **Palette:** red, black, off-white/cream.
- **Typography:** bold condensed or geometric sans-serif with clear supporting labels.
- **Layout/grid:** diagonal energy balanced by rectangular writing boxes; strong directional reading path.
- **Cover composition:** diagonal composition leading toward the title, with one historically appropriate symbol.
- **Decoration:** arrows, rays, circles, blocks, simple line patterns.
- **Best subjects:** revolutions, political history, social movements, civics.
- **Avoid:** using symbols without context or tilting actual handwriting boxes so the student cannot write comfortably.

### 10. Memphis Design — playful 1980s geometry
- **Visual signature:** squiggles, checkerboards, zigzags, terrazzo dots, irregular shapes.
- **Palette:** turquoise, coral, lavender, yellow, black, off-white.
- **Typography:** chunky geometric display plus clean small labels.
- **Layout/grid:** playful asymmetry around stable, aligned writing boxes.
- **Cover composition:** large title paired with an arrangement of simple irregular geometric objects.
- **Decoration:** squiggles, dots, patterns and tiny checkerboards in the margins.
- **Best subjects:** art, creative writing, activity books, mathematics activities, exhibitions.
- **Avoid:** placing noisy patterns behind handwriting or reusing the same colour-block look as the previous project.

### 11. Dark Academia — scholarly and literary
- **Visual signature:** antique book motifs, architecture, marginal notes, paper texture, restrained classical detail.
- **Palette:** espresso brown, parchment, burgundy, olive, charcoal.
- **Typography:** book serif titles and small clean labels; optional handwritten annotation accents.
- **Layout/grid:** book-page framing, chapter-like headings, broad text zones.
- **Cover composition:** classic title arrangement with one literary, historical, or academic illustration.
- **Decoration:** thin frames, small stars, archival stamps only when meaningful, classical line art.
- **Best subjects:** English, history, philosophy, literary analysis, biographies.
- **Avoid:** making the page too dark to print cleanly or using faux handwriting for the actual writing zones.

### 12. Editorial Magazine — modern and premium
- **Visual signature:** oversized headline, precise captions, image blocks, strong whitespace, editorial rhythm.
- **Palette:** black/white plus one intentional accent such as cobalt, orange, forest green, or burgundy.
- **Typography:** expressive display headline with a neutral sans-serif or serif supporting face.
- **Layout/grid:** magazine-style columns, image/text modules, folio or section labels where useful.
- **Cover composition:** headline occupies one side or top third, with a large photograph-like illustration or cutout on the other side.
- **Decoration:** fine rules, small captions, numbered sections; no fake publication logos.
- **Best subjects:** almost any subject that needs a sophisticated, contemporary look.
- **Avoid:** narrow columns that are unsuitable for handwriting or making a school project look like an actual branded magazine.

### 13. Neo-Brutalism — bold contemporary blocks
- **Visual signature:** heavy borders, flat blocks, visible structure, high-contrast labels, deliberate rawness.
- **Palette:** acid yellow, cobalt, black, white, optional bright pink or orange.
- **Typography:** bold sans-serif and blocky labels.
- **Layout/grid:** large rectilinear cards, clear separation, thick outlines; slightly playful but aligned.
- **Cover composition:** large title card paired with a simple cutout or icon.
- **Decoration:** hard-edge blocks, oversized numbers, simple arrows.
- **Best subjects:** AI, computing, entrepreneurship, economics, modern technology.
- **Avoid:** clutter, gratuitous overlaps, too many harsh shadows, or low-contrast text over bright blocks.

### 14. Botanical Field Journal — naturalist and handmade
- **Visual signature:** labelled plant drawings, field-note structure, leaf silhouettes, specimen tabs, natural textures.
- **Palette:** sage, moss green, beige, terracotta, dark brown.
- **Typography:** clear serif or plain sans-serif, with small label-like captions.
- **Layout/grid:** specimen illustration on one side, broad writing box on the other; consistent labelled sections.
- **Cover composition:** one prominent botanical or geographic specimen with a neat title panel.
- **Decoration:** small leaves, fine-line plants, specimen numbers and subtle notebook rules.
- **Best subjects:** biology, ecology, geography, agriculture, environmental studies.
- **Avoid:** generic leaves on every unrelated project or illustrations that use writing space.

### 15. Mughal Manuscript / Indo-Persian — ornate cultural heritage
- **Visual signature:** arch-shaped frames, fine floral ornament, miniature-painting influence, elaborate margins, balanced symmetry.
- **Palette:** deep indigo, maroon, cream, emerald, antique-gold simulation.
- **Typography:** elegant serif-inspired Latin titles, clear readable labels; authentic scripts only when provided and verified.
- **Layout/grid:** central framed area with decorated outer margins; writing boxes remain geometrically practical.
- **Cover composition:** title inside a refined arch or panel with a culturally appropriate motif.
- **Decoration:** floral arabesques, fine linework, decorative corners, restrained gold-like detail.
- **Best subjects:** Indian history, Urdu/Hindi literature, architecture, art, cultural studies.
- **Avoid:** fake Arabic/Urdu lettering, stereotyped cultural symbols, overcrowded ornament, or inaccurate historical motifs.

## 10. Subject-to-theme matching guide

This is a starting recommendation, not a mandatory mapping. Choose based on the exact topic and requested mood.

| Subject / project type | Strong first choices | Other good choices |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Bauhaus; Swiss International | Neo-Brutalism; Editorial Magazine |
| Physics | Space-Age Futurism; Swiss International | Bauhaus; Editorial Magazine |
| Chemistry | Victorian Scientific Journal; Art Deco | Swiss International; Editorial Magazine |
| Biology | Botanical Field Journal; Art Nouveau | Victorian Scientific Journal; Japanese Minimalism |
| Environmental science | Botanical Field Journal; Japanese Minimalism | Art Nouveau; Editorial Magazine |
| History | Dark Academia; Constructivism | Mughal Manuscript; Art Deco; Editorial Magazine |
| Social Studies / Civics | Constructivism; Editorial Magazine | Swiss International; Pop Art |
| Geography | Japanese Minimalism; Botanical Field Journal | Editorial Magazine; Victorian Scientific Journal |
| English literature / poetry | Dark Academia; Art Nouveau | Editorial Magazine; Japanese Minimalism |
| Hindi / Urdu | Mughal Manuscript; Editorial Magazine | Dark Academia; Art Nouveau |
| Computer Science / AI | Neo-Brutalism; Swiss International | Space-Age Futurism; Bauhaus |
| Business / entrepreneurship | Art Deco; Editorial Magazine | Swiss International; Neo-Brutalism |
| Health / road safety / awareness | Pop Art; Swiss International | Constructivism; Editorial Magazine |
| Art / design | Memphis; Bauhaus | Art Deco; Art Nouveau; Editorial Magazine |
| School exhibition / creative project | Memphis; Editorial Magazine | Pop Art; Neo-Brutalism; Bauhaus |

**Rotation rule:** Do not choose the first option automatically every time. Check previous projects and choose a style with a visibly different palette, typography, layout, and decoration.
